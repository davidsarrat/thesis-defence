The closing renders the exact phrase “Thank you.” locally at preparation time. The letter shapes remain the
font's original outlines. Original hand-authored centreline curves follow the Italianno glyphs and reveal them
through round nib masks; the font file itself is neither edited nor subsetted. There are no external font
requests during the presentation.

The centreline study separates the T's top and stem from the h's high ascender curl, which belongs to the h in
Italianno. The reveal then follows the joined `hank`, the y and its leftward descender curl, joined `ou`, and the
full stop. The ink is `#151216`, with no outline, shadow, glow or global opacity fade.
