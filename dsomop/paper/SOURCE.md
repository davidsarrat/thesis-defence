# dsOMOP's paper (O20)

`dsomop-bioinformatics-header.webp`: the top of the published paper's first page, shown on O20's click (David, 9 Nov
2026: «una ventana 2D enseñando el paper dsOMOP de OUP Bioinformatics… que se vea lo que se vería así en la web,
estético, si sobra algo quítalo»).

- **Paper:** Sarrat-González D, Escribà-Montagut X, Houghtaling J, González JR. dsOMOP: bridging OMOP CDM and DataSHIELD
  for secure federated analysis of standardized clinical data. Bioinformatics 41(6):btaf286, 2025.
  doi:10.1093/bioinformatics/btaf286. Open access, CC BY 4.0, © The Author(s), published by Oxford University Press.
- **Source:** the publisher's PDF from the PMC Article Datasets (PMC12187060.1, `pmc-oa-opendata` on AWS Open Data;
  md5 97a7a9647eed4dafdcab286832daf299). The PDF stays out of git.
- **Made:** two bands of page 1 rendered at 300 dpi with PyMuPDF: the masthead to the affiliations, then the abstract's
  Motivation and Results. Left out: the corresponding author, the associate editor, «Availability and implementation»
  and the introduction. Saved as lossless WebP (2209 × 1536).
